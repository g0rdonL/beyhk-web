How the rating works

BeyHK uses Glicko-2: every player has a rating and a deviation (1764 ± 51, say). New players have a wide deviation, so their rating moves fast; the more you play, the narrower it gets and the steadier your rating. Idle time widens it again.

  • Confirmed matches only. Until the opponent confirms, neither rating moves.
  • Big wins count for more. A 4–0 or an Xtreme finish scales both players’ change by up to 1.75×.
  • Five placement matches. After five confirmed matches your name appears on the ladder.
  • Sorted by rating. One Hong Kong ladder, with a view per district.

Seasons

The ladder runs in seasons. Season 0 runs from launch until 1 January 2027; after that, one season per calendar quarter. At season end ratings move halfway back to 1500 and deviations widen, so everyone has something to play for.

Everyone gets a season recap: rank, tier, record, best streak, most-played blade.

Why the ladder holds up

Two-party confirmation

Your opponent confirms by scanning a QR code on the spot, or later from a push notification. Unanswered logs expire after 48 hours.

Witness or referee

Name a witness, or let a shop referee confirm outright.

Disputes

Attach a photo and raise a dispute for moderators to settle. Server-side rate limits apply.
