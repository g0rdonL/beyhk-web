By Hong Kong players, for Hong Kong players

BeyHK (螺Fun) is an unofficial community project. It sets out to do one thing: a real, opponent-confirmed head-to-head ranking for the city, and the community around it.

What we stand by

Opponent-confirmed

Ratings move only when the opponent confirms. Nobody enters their own results.

No ads, ever

No ad network, no banners, no sponsor slots, no paid ordering.

Hong Kong

One ladder for the city with a view per district; Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) first, English alongside.

Safe by default

Minors are assumed present: block, report, filter and moderators from day one; under-13s through a parent-managed account.

Unofficial

BeyHK is not affiliated with Takara Tomy or Hasbro. Beyblade is a trademark of its owners. The app uses no official logos or product art; we are “for Beyblade X”.

Contact

The quickest way is Instagram @beyhkfun or Threads. Reports and contact inside the app reach the moderators.
