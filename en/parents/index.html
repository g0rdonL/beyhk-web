Parents & safety

Your kid wants to rank? You decide what’s on

BeyHK is for players aged 13 and over. Under 13, a parent creates a managed account with their own email and uses a guardian PIN to decide what is on and what stays off.

Privacy policyTerms of use

Managed accounts

  1. The parent creates the account

    The age question at sign-up is answered by the parent for themselves, with the parent’s email.

  2. Set a guardian PIN

    The PIN makes it a managed account. Chat, direct messages, photos, the meet-up check and the district view all start off.

  3. Turn things on, one at a time

    The parent uses the PIN to choose what is on. It can be changed at any time.

Chat safety

  • Minors are assumed present. Every room and every direct message has block, report, a profanity filter and moderator removal.
  • Direct messages are limited. Only two players with a confirmed match together can message each other; off by default on managed accounts.
  • Photos are reviewed. Avatars go public only after a moderator approves them. Match-proof photos are visible only to that match’s participants and moderators, and are deleted 30 days after the match.

Location and privacy

Location

Used only during a meet-up check you agreed to (two minutes at most), and only “within 50 m or not” is kept, never a position or a distance.

Analytics

Usage statistics are collected only if you opt in. No advertising id.

No ads

No ad network, no banners, no sponsor slots.

Always

Delete the account

Settings → Delete account, or see the delete your data page.

What BeyHK never does

Never

Sells to kids

The app is free with no in-app purchases. Every versus skin is unlocked by playing, and nothing is sold to under-13 accounts.

Never

Shares a position

The meet-up check shares only “close enough or not”.

Never

Tracks you

Data is not used for ad tracking and is not sold to third parties.

The details are in the privacy policy and the terms of use.
