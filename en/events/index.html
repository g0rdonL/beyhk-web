For shops and organizers

Shops and events run tournaments in the app: single elimination, or Swiss rounds with an optional top 4 / top 8 cut. Organizer-named judges record results; champion and standings appear immediately.

  • Tournament results never touch the ladder. The ranking counts only confirmed head-to-head matches between players; tournaments stand on their own.
  • Judges hold a graded licence reviewed by moderators. A shop referee can also confirm ladder matches played on site.
  • No ads. No sponsor slots and no paid ordering; every shop sits on the map on equal terms.

Want to run a tournament at your shop? Find us on Instagram @beyhkfun.
