Why BeyHK

Ranking

A rating that only moves when your opponent confirms

Glicko-2 rating (rating ± deviation), confirmed by QR on the spot or from a push. Five placement matches put you on the ladder with a Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum tier, city-wide and by district.

Community

Rooms for Hong Kong players

A Hong Kong-wide room, a room per shop and one per tier. Challenge someone straight from chat and the log opens pre-filled. Block, report, a profanity filter and moderators built in.

Events

Shops and tournaments, in the app

A map of shops and venues and the upcoming events. Organizers run single-elimination or Swiss tournaments in the app with judges recording results. Tournament results never touch the ladder.

Season 0 is open

Season 0 runs from launch until 1 January 2027. At season end ratings move halfway back to 1500 and everyone gets a season recap: rank, tier, record, best streak, most-played blade. Seasons then run by calendar quarter.

For parents
