Why BeyHK

Ranking

A rating that only moves when your opponent confirms

Glicko-2 rating (rating ± deviation), confirmed by QR on the spot or from a push. Five placement matches put you on the ladder with a Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum tier, city-wide and by district.

Community

Rooms for Hong Kong players

A Hong Kong-wide room, a room per shop and one per tier. Challenge someone straight from chat and the log opens pre-filled. Block, report, a profanity filter and moderators built in.

Events

Shops and tournaments, in the app

A map of shops and venues and the upcoming events. Organizers run single-elimination or Swiss tournaments in the app with judges recording results. Tournament results never touch the ladder.

How to start: three steps to the ladder

  1. Create an account

    Aged 13 or over, sign up yourself; under 13, a parent sets up a managed account.

  2. Play 5 placement matches

    After each match your opponent confirms by QR scan or push, within 48 hours.

  3. Get on the ladder

    Earn a Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum tier and see where you rank in Hong Kong and your district.

Questions

Is BeyHK an official app?

No. BeyHK is the unofficial community app for Beyblade X players in Hong Kong and is not affiliated with Takara Tomy or Hasbro. Beyblade is a trademark of its owners.

Does BeyHK cost anything?

No. BeyHK is free, has no ads and has no in-app purchases.

How does the ranking work?

BeyHK uses Glicko-2: every player has a rating and a deviation (±). A rating only moves after the opponent confirms the match by QR scan or push. Five placement matches put you on the ladder, in one of four tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum), city-wide and by district.

How long does my opponent have to confirm a match?

Your opponent has 48 hours to confirm. The rating only changes once they do.

How do I find opponents?

The app has a Hong Kong-wide chat room, a room for every shop and one for every tier. Challenge someone straight from chat; once they accept, the match log opens.

Do tournament results count towards the ranking?

No. Organizers can run single-elimination or Swiss tournaments in the app with judges recording results on the spot, but tournament results never touch the ranking.

When does the season end, and what happens then?

Season 0 runs from launch until 1 January 2027. At season end ratings move halfway back to 1500 and everyone gets a season recap: rank, tier, record, best streak and most-played blade. Seasons then run by calendar quarter.

What age is BeyHK for, and what can parents control?

BeyHK is for players aged 13 and over. A player under 13 needs a parent to set up a managed account with the parent’s own email; a guardian PIN then decides whether chat, direct messages, friends, photos and the meet-up check are on.

When does BeyHK launch?

BeyHK is in a closed beta and you can sign up to join. The iOS and Android versions are coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How can shops and tournament organizers use BeyHK?

Every shop has its own chat room in the app, and shops and venues appear on the map. Organizers can run single-elimination or Swiss tournaments in the app, with judges recording results on the spot.

Glossary

H2H match
A one-on-one match (head-to-head).
Opponent confirmation
Your opponent confirms the result by QR scan or push; only then does the match count towards the ranking.
Placement matches
Your first 5 matches, which set your starting rank and tier.
Tier
The four levels of the ladder: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum.
Glicko-2
The rating system BeyHK uses. Besides a rating it tracks a deviation: the more you play, the smaller the deviation and the more accurate your rank.
Deviation (±)
How sure the system is about your rating. The smaller the number, the more accurate the rating.
Single elimination
Lose once and you are out; winners advance.
Swiss rounds
Each round pairs players with similar records. A loss does not knock you out; final places are set after a fixed number of rounds.
Season reset
At season end every rating moves halfway back to 1500.
