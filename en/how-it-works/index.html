How it works

Log · Confirm · Climb

Five steps from sign-up to the ladder.

Get the appInstagram @beyhkfun

Five steps

  1. Create an account

    Sign in with an email code, Apple or Google. Players are 13 and over; under-13s through a parent-managed account.

  2. Set up your profile

    Pick a handle, add an avatar (public after moderator review) and optionally a district. Type your deck in go-shoot shorthand, like DrSw 3-60 F.

  3. Log the match after you play

    Enter the score (first to 4, first to 3 or a single round), round by round if you like: spin, over, burst and Xtreme finishes.

  4. Your opponent confirms

    They scan your QR code on the spot or confirm later from a push notification. Unanswered logs expire after 48 hours. Name a witness, or let a shop referee confirm.

  5. Rank

    After five confirmed matches you appear on the Hong Kong ladder with a tier.

Meet-up check and line-up

Optional

Meet-up check (MET)

Before a match, a two-minute check proves you were together: both phones must be within 50 m, one shows a number and the other picks it. The match card then carries a MET badge. Only “close enough or not” is shared, never a position or a distance.

Optional

Pre-match line-up

Both players lock their beys, reveal together, order the launches and confirm each other’s physical line-up. Gold and above can play 4 ban 1.

Also in the app

  • Decks and parts: track the parts you own as a collection, scan a shop’s BeyHK product code to add a whole box in one tap, see win rates per part and per combo, and the Hong Kong 30-day median price players report.
  • Achievements: nineteen badges the app awards as you earn them.
  • Versus skins: the versus screen’s entrance, line colour and glow are yours to mix — every skin is unlocked by playing, none is sold.
  • Friends and invites: share your invite link beyhk.sh/i/… and whoever accepts becomes a friend, one tap from a challenge.
