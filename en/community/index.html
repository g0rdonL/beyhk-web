In the app

Rooms

A Hong Kong-wide room, a room per shop and one per tier. Tier rooms open after your five placement matches.

Challenges

Challenge someone straight from chat; when they accept, the match log opens pre-filled.

Direct messages

Only with players you have a confirmed match with. Off by default on managed accounts, at the guardian’s choice.

Friends and invites

Share your invite link beyhk.sh/i/… and whoever accepts becomes a friend. Opponent not on the app yet? The same link brings them in, and their first confirmed match earns you a referrer badge.

Safety

The rooms assume minors are present: block, report, a profanity filter and moderator removal are there from day one. Parents & safety
