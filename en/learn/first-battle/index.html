One scan starts your first battle

Two players stand together, one phone scans the other, and an unrated battle starts.

Get the appInstagram @beyhkfun

The pictures

1

Two players, two phones

Just stand together. No typing a name, no waiting for a notification, no accept tap.

2

Tap Casual on the Rank tab

Both players tap “Casual · 掃描即一般對戰”. The phone opens its scanner.

3

A shows “my QR”

A taps “出示我的 QR” under the camera. The code lasts 2 minutes and renews itself while it is showing.

4

B scans it

B points the scanner at A’s code. The scan is the accept, and both phones land in the battle.

5

Each brings 3 beys

Each player submits a line-up of 3 beys. Both are revealed at once, then you check each other’s.

6

One person keeps score

The system picks a scorer. Only the scorer writes the score; the other phone follows along.

7

Unrated, so just play

A casual battle never touches your rating. It settles into a match record, and you can rematch at once if both want to.

Words

Casual
An unrated battle. It never changes your rating.
My QR
A battle code that lasts 2 minutes. It is not your profile QR.
Line-up
The 3 beys you bring to this battle.
Scorer
The one player the system picks to write the score.

Check yourself

Who shows the QR and who scans?

One player taps “出示我的 QR” and the other scans it. The scan is the accept, with no confirm step.

Will losing lower my rating?

No. Casual battles are unrated and never touch your rating.

What if a phone goes offline mid-battle?

Battles are live, so both phones stay online. Five minutes offline ends the battle early.
