How the ranking works

A rated match changes your rating and rank only after both players confirm it.

Get the appInstagram @beyhkfun

The pictures

1

After the battle, log it

Either player can log it. Three things go in: opponent, decks, score.

2

Your opponent confirms

Your opponent confirms, rejects or disputes it from the inbox. In person, a QR scan confirms on the spot.

3

There are 48 hours

Unconfirmed after 48 hours, the match expires and never counts.

4

Confirmed, then the rating moves

Only confirmed matches move your rating. Ratings are worked out with Glicko-2.

5

Five matches to get on

You appear on the ladder after 5 confirmed matches. Until then you see “placement 3/5”.

6

Four tiers

Tiers run from Bronze to Silver, Gold and Platinum.

7

Same opponent, less each time

Against the same opponent, matches from the 3rd confirmed one within 24 hours count less.

8

A new season starts over

A new season starts ratings over. When one closes you get a recap: rank, peak rating, win rate, best streak.

Words

Rating
A Glicko-2 number that goes up or down after a confirmed match.
Confirmed
Both players have agreed on the result.
Placement
The 5 confirmed matches before you appear on the ladder.
Tier
One of Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum.
Dispute
Your opponent disagrees with the result. A moderator handles it.
Season
A stretch of play. A new season starts ratings over.

Check yourself

I logged a match. When does my rating change?

After your opponent confirms. Only confirmed matches move your rating.

What if my opponent never confirms?

Unconfirmed after 48 hours, it expires and never counts.

Why am I not on the ladder yet?

You appear after 5 confirmed matches. Until then you see progress such as “placement 3/5”.
