我哋收集乜嘢

  • 帳戶：電郵（或 Apple / Google 登入嘅識別碼）、帳號（handle）、顯示名稱、頭像、地區（可選）、語言。
  • 對戰：你記錄或確認嘅對戰結果、分數、場地、使用嘅套裝，以及由此計算嘅評分同排名。排名同對戰紀錄會向其他用戶顯示。
  • 聊天：你喺聊天室發送嘅訊息、同對戰過嘅玩家之間嘅私訊、你嘅封鎖同檢舉。訊息會經過粗口過濾，版主可以刪除；私訊只有你哋兩位同（有人檢舉之後）版主睇到。
  • 相片：你上載嘅頭像（版主批准後先公開）同對戰證明相片（只限該場對戰嘅參與者同版主睇到，對戰完結 30 日後刪除）。
  • 見面確認嘅位置：只喺你同意進行嘅見面確認期間（最多兩分鐘、只限使用 App 時）傳送精確位置；確認完結後位置即刪除，只保留兩部手機嘅距離。對方永遠睇唔到你嘅位置。
  • 發射力：你用藍牙連接 Beybattle Pass 時記錄嘅發射力（SP）數值。
  • 裝置：推送通知 token（如你開啟通知）、App 版本同作業系統。
  • 穩定性同使用數據：當機報告同效能數據（Sentry，只含用戶 ID，唔含電郵）同少量使用事件（例如「已記錄對戰」，同你喺邊幾日開過 App，連同用戶 ID），用嚟改善 App。
  • 使用數據（只限你開啟咗）：預設關閉。如果你喺「設定 › 私隱 › 分享使用數據」開啟（受管理帳戶只可以由監護人更改），App 會將你開過邊啲畫面、撳過邊啲掣、捲動幾多同用過邊啲掃動操作傳去 Google Analytics for Firebase。呢啲事件唔包括用戶 ID、名稱、訊息或者位置，亦唔會收集廣告 ID；Google Analytics 會由連線嘅 IP 地址推算大概位置（城市同國家）。關閉之後即停止收集，並清走呢部手機上嘅數據。
  • 網站（beyhk.sh）分析（只限你接受咗）：第一次開呢個網站會問你接受定拒絕 Cookie。你撳「接受」之後，網站先會經 Google Tag Manager 載入 Google Analytics，記錄你睇咗邊啲頁、由邊度連過嚟、裝置同瀏覽器類型，同埋由 IP 地址推算嘅大概位置（城市同國家），並喺你嘅瀏覽器存 Google Analytics 嘅 Cookie。所有廣告用途（廣告 Cookie、廣告個人化、Google 信號）一律關閉。邀請連結（beyhk.sh/i/…）嘅頁面唔會載入分析，所以邀請碼唔會送去 Google。你可以隨時喺網站頁底「Cookie 設定」改變主意；揀拒絕會清走呢啲 Cookie。網站分析同 App 嘅使用數據分開處理，唔會連到你嘅 BeyHK 帳戶。
  • 舉報分析：當有玩家舉報訊息或玩家時，以下資料會交由騰訊雲 TokenHub 上嘅智譜 GLM 模型自動分析（喺新加坡處理），為管理員提供建議：被舉報帳戶嘅用戶名、顯示名稱同陣容名稱；被舉報嘅訊息及同一聊天室前後嘅訊息（連同發訊者嘅用戶名同時間）；如屬舉報玩家，則為被舉報玩家最近嘅訊息（連同時間；私訊只限已有訊息被舉報嘅對話）；舉報人填寫嘅說明、舉報類別同時間；舉報人過往舉報嘅成立／駁回紀錄；雙方最近 48 小時內有冇爭議、拒絕或敗仗，以及有冇互相封鎖；以及被舉報帳戶嘅開戶日數、是否家長管理帳戶、是否停權、開放中嘅舉報數目、過往個案同處分、段位。唔會送出電郵地址、帳戶 ID 或位置。分析結果只有管理員睇到，唔會自動處分任何人；涉及兒童安全嘅舉報唔會送出分析。騰訊雲按其服務條款處理呢啲資料。

我哋點樣用

提供排名同對戰確認、發送與你對戰相關嘅通知、維持聊天室安全、修正錯誤同了解功能使用情況。我哋唔會出售你嘅資料，亦冇廣告網絡。

邊個可以睇到

資料儲存喺 Supabase（資料庫同檔案儲存）。當機報告由 Sentry 處理。推送通知經 Expo 送出。如果你開啟咗分享使用數據，相關數據由 Google Analytics for Firebase 處理；如果你喺網站接受咗 Cookie，網站分析由 Google Analytics 處理。舉報分析由 騰訊雲 TokenHub（智譜 GLM 模型，新加坡）為管理員處理。以上服務只為運作 App 而處理資料。

年齡

BeyHK 供 13 歲或以上人士使用；註冊時需要確認年齡。13 歲以下嘅玩家只可以用家長或監護人開設同管理嘅帳戶（受管理帳戶）：聊天、私訊、上載相片同顯示地區預設關閉，要監護人 PIN 先可以開啟。聊天室預設有未成年人在場，因此提供封鎖、檢舉、粗口過濾同版主刪除功能。

保留同刪除

你可以隨時喺「設定 → 刪除帳戶」刪除帳戶：登入資料、個人檔案、訊息、套裝同物品會被刪除；對戰紀錄會匿名化為「已刪除玩家」以保持其他玩家嘅排名正確。詳見刪除資料。

What we collect

  • Account: email (or the identifier from Apple / Google sign-in), handle, display name, avatar, district (optional), language.
  • Matches: results you log or confirm, scores, venue, decks used, and the rating and rank computed from them. Rankings and match history are visible to other users.
  • Chat: messages you send in rooms, direct messages with players you have played, and your blocks and reports. Messages pass a profanity filter and can be removed by moderators; a direct message is seen only by the two of you and, once someone reports it, moderators.
  • Photos: the avatar you upload (public only after a moderator approves it) and match-proof photos (seen only by that match's players and moderators, deleted 30 days after the match settles).
  • Location for the meet-up check: your precise location, only during a meet-up check you agree to (at most two minutes, only while the app is open). The positions are deleted when the check ends; only the distance between the two phones is kept. The other player never sees your position.
  • Shoot power: the shoot-power (SP) readings saved when you connect a Beybattle Pass over Bluetooth.
  • Device: a push notification token (if you enable notifications), app version and OS.
  • Stability and usage: crash reports and performance data (Sentry, with your user id, never your email) and a small set of usage events with your user id (for example "match logged", and which days you open the app) to improve the app.
  • Usage stats (only if you turn them on): off by default. If you switch on Settings › Privacy › Share usage stats (on a managed account only the guardian can), the app sends Google Analytics for Firebase which screens you open and which buttons you tap, how far you scroll and which swipe actions you use. These events carry no user id, handle, messages or location, and no advertising id is collected; Google Analytics works out an approximate location (city and country) from the connection's IP address. Switching it off stops collection and clears the data held on your phone.
  • Website (beyhk.sh) analytics (only if you accept): the first time you open the site it asks you to accept or reject cookies. Only after you tap Accept does the site load Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager, which records which pages you view, where you came from, your device and browser type and an approximate location (city and country) worked out from your IP address, and stores Google Analytics cookies in your browser. Every advertising use (ad cookies, ad personalisation, Google signals) is off. Invite-link pages (beyhk.sh/i/…) never load analytics, so invite codes are not sent to Google. You can change your mind at any time from "Cookie settings" at the foot of every page; rejecting clears those cookies. Website analytics is kept apart from the app's usage stats and is not linked to your BeyHK account.
  • Report analysis: When a player reports a message or a player, the following is analysed by the Zhipu GLM model hosted on Tencent Cloud TokenHub (processed in Singapore) to give moderators a suggestion: the reported account's handle, display name and deck names; the reported message and nearby messages in the same room, with their senders' handles and times; for a player report, the reported player's recent messages with their times (direct messages only from conversations that already have a reported message); the reporter's note, the report category and time; the reporter's record of upheld and dismissed reports; whether the two players had a dispute, rejection or loss in the previous 48 hours, and whether either has blocked the other; and the reported account's age in days, whether it is a parent-managed account, whether it is banned, the number of open reports against the account, its past cases and actions, and its tier. No email address, account id or location is sent. Only moderators see the result and nothing is actioned automatically; child-safety reports are never sent. Tencent Cloud processes this data under its service terms.

How we use it

To run the ranking and match confirmation, send notifications about your matches, keep chat safe, fix bugs and understand which features are used. We do not sell your data and there is no ad network.

Who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage). Crash reports are handled by Sentry. Push notifications are delivered through Expo. If you opted in to usage stats, they are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase; if you accepted cookies on the website, its analytics are processed by Google Analytics. Report triage for moderators is processed by Tencent Cloud TokenHub (Zhipu GLM model, Singapore). These providers process data only to operate the app.

Age

BeyHK is for people aged 13 and over; you confirm this at sign-up. A player under 13 can only use an account that a parent or guardian sets up and keeps (a managed account): chat, direct messages, photo uploads and showing a district start off, and only the guardian PIN turns them on. Chat assumes minors are present, so blocking, reporting, a profanity filter and moderator deletion are always on.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account: your login, profile, messages, decks and inventory are removed; match history is anonymised as "Deleted player" so other players' rankings stay correct. See Delete your data.
