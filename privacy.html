我哋收集乜嘢

  • 帳戶：電郵（或 Apple / Google 登入嘅識別碼）、帳號（handle）、顯示名稱、頭像、地區（可選）、語言。
  • 對戰：你記錄或確認嘅對戰結果、分數、場地、使用嘅套裝，以及由此計算嘅評分同排名。排名同對戰紀錄會向其他用戶顯示。
  • 聊天：你喺聊天室發送嘅訊息、同對戰過嘅玩家之間嘅私訊、你嘅封鎖同檢舉。訊息會經過粗口過濾，版主可以刪除；私訊只有你哋兩位同（有人檢舉之後）版主睇到。
  • 相片：你上載嘅頭像（版主批准後先公開）同對戰證明相片（只限該場對戰嘅參與者同版主睇到，對戰完結 30 日後刪除）。
  • 見面確認嘅位置：只喺你同意進行嘅見面確認期間（最多兩分鐘、只限使用 App 時）傳送精確位置；確認完結後位置即刪除，只保留兩部手機嘅距離。對方永遠睇唔到你嘅位置。
  • 發射力：你用藍牙連接 Beybattle Pass 時記錄嘅發射力（SP）數值。
  • 裝置：推送通知 token（如你開啟通知）、App 版本同作業系統。
  • 穩定性同使用數據：當機報告同效能數據（Sentry，只含用戶 ID，唔含電郵）同少量使用事件（例如「已記錄對戰」，連同用戶 ID），用嚟改善 App。
  • 贊助位：贊助位嘅顯示次數同點擊次數。已登入用戶只記錄用戶 ID；未登入用戶唔會被識別。

我哋點樣用

提供排名同對戰確認、發送與你對戰相關嘅通知、維持聊天室安全、修正錯誤同了解功能使用情況。我哋唔會出售你嘅資料，亦冇廣告網絡。

邊個可以睇到

資料儲存喺 Supabase（資料庫同檔案儲存）。當機報告由 Sentry 處理。推送通知經 Expo 送出。以上服務只為運作 App 而處理資料。

年齡

BeyHK 供 13 歲或以上人士使用；註冊時需要確認年齡。13 歲以下嘅玩家只可以用家長或監護人開設同管理嘅帳戶（受管理帳戶）：聊天、私訊、上載相片同顯示地區預設關閉，要監護人 PIN 先可以開啟。聊天室預設有未成年人在場，因此提供封鎖、檢舉、粗口過濾同版主刪除功能。

保留同刪除

你可以隨時喺「設定 → 刪除帳戶」刪除帳戶：登入資料、個人檔案、訊息、套裝同物品會被刪除；對戰紀錄會匿名化為「已刪除玩家」以保持其他玩家嘅排名正確。詳見刪除資料。

What we collect

  • Account: email (or the identifier from Apple / Google sign-in), handle, display name, avatar, district (optional), language.
  • Matches: results you log or confirm, scores, venue, decks used, and the rating and rank computed from them. Rankings and match history are visible to other users.
  • Chat: messages you send in rooms, direct messages with players you have played, and your blocks and reports. Messages pass a profanity filter and can be removed by moderators; a direct message is seen only by the two of you and, once someone reports it, moderators.
  • Photos: the avatar you upload (public only after a moderator approves it) and match-proof photos (seen only by that match's players and moderators, deleted 30 days after the match settles).
  • Location for the meet-up check: your precise location, only during a meet-up check you agree to (at most two minutes, only while the app is open). The positions are deleted when the check ends; only the distance between the two phones is kept. The other player never sees your position.
  • Shoot power: the shoot-power (SP) readings saved when you connect a Beybattle Pass over Bluetooth.
  • Device: a push notification token (if you enable notifications), app version and OS.
  • Stability and usage: crash reports and performance data (Sentry, with your user id, never your email) and a small set of usage events with your user id (for example "match logged") to improve the app.
  • Sponsor slot: how often a sponsor creative was shown or tapped. Signed-in users are recorded by user id only; anonymous viewers are not identified.

How we use it

To run the ranking and match confirmation, send notifications about your matches, keep chat safe, fix bugs and understand which features are used. We do not sell your data and there is no ad network.

Who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage). Crash reports are handled by Sentry. Push notifications are delivered through Expo. These providers process data only to operate the app.

Age

BeyHK is for people aged 13 and over; you confirm this at sign-up. A player under 13 can only use an account that a parent or guardian sets up and keeps (a managed account): chat, direct messages, photo uploads and showing a district start off, and only the guardian PIN turns them on. Chat assumes minors are present, so blocking, reporting, a profanity filter and moderator deletion are always on.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account: your login, profile, messages, decks and inventory are removed; match history is anonymised as "Deleted player" so other players' rankings stay correct. See Delete your data.
